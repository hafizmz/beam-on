    Physicist's notebook

    Two rules decide every reading on the meter:

    dose = S ÷ r² × (½)ⁿ

    Inverse square law: r is the distance from the linac in metres. Double the distance and the dose drops to a quarter.
    Half-value layers: n counts how many times the wall halves the beam. A metre of concrete is 2 HVL here; lead is 6.

    Game simplifications: beam strength is scaled down, each block is 1 m, and scatter around the maze is ignored. Real bunkers are designed with tenth-value layers using methods such as NCRP Report 151.
