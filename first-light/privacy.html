English

Who collects your details

Beam On: First Light is a free medical physics outreach game. Your details are collected by the organiser, Mohd Hafiz Mohd Zin. Contact: hafiz.zin@gmail.com.

What we collect, and whether you must give it

First name or nicknameNeeded to play. A nickname is fine.
School, state, Form or yearNeeded to play. They show which schools and age groups the programme reaches.
Interest rating (1–5)Needed to play. Asked before and after the game, to measure whether it helps.
Age group (under 18, or 18 or over)Needed to play. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree before you send your details.
EmailOptional. Asked only if you choose to hear about outreach events.
Feedback and stars earnedOptional. Sent only if you choose "Tell us how it went".

If you do not give the required details, you cannot play the game. Saying no to email has no effect on the game.

Why we use it

  • To run and improve this outreach programme.
  • To report anonymous totals, such as the number of schools reached and the change in interest. No individual is named in reports.
  • If you ticked the box, to email you about medical physics outreach events.

We do not sell your details, use them for advertising, or share them with anyone else.

Where it is stored

The game keeps your details in your own browser. They reach the organiser only when you press Submit on the Google Form. Google Forms stores responses on Google's servers, which may be outside Malaysia. By submitting, you agree to this transfer. Only the organiser can see the responses.

How long we keep it

Responses are kept until 31 December 2027 and then deleted. Anonymous totals may be kept longer.

Your rights

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your agreement to emails, by writing to hafiz.zin@gmail.com.
  • Remove the copy in your browser at any time with "Clear progress on this device" on the game's menu page.

Players under 18

If you are under 18, please show this notice to a parent or guardian and ask them before you send your details. A parent or guardian can contact the organiser to see or delete their child's details.

E-melPilihan. Ditanya hanya jika anda mahu menerima berita program jangkauan.

Tujuan penggunaan

  • Untuk menjalankan dan menambah baik program jangkauan ini.
  • Untuk melaporkan jumlah tanpa nama, seperti bilangan sekolah dan perubahan tahap minat. Tiada individu dinamakan dalam laporan.

Kami tidak menjual maklumat anda, menggunakannya untuk pengiklanan, atau berkongsi dengan pihak lain.

Tempat penyimpanan

Permainan ini menyimpan maklumat anda dalam pelayar anda sendiri. Maklumat hanya sampai kepada penganjur apabila anda menekan Submit pada Borang Google. Borang Google menyimpan jawapan di pelayan Google yang mungkin berada di luar Malaysia. Dengan menghantar borang, anda bersetuju dengan pemindahan ini. Hanya penganjur boleh melihat jawapan.

Tempoh penyimpanan

Jawapan disimpan sehingga 31 Disember 2027 dan kemudian dipadam. Jumlah tanpa nama mungkin disimpan lebih lama.
