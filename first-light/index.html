How to become a medical physicist

The route in Malaysia, step by step.

  1. SPM or equivalentO level or IGCSE also count. Take Physics and Additional Mathematics; Chemistry and Biology help too.
  2. Pre-universitySTPM, matriculation, foundation, A levels or the IB in science.
  3. Bachelor's degreeBSc in Physics or Medical Physics.
  4. MSc Medical PhysicsRadiation physics, dosimetry, imaging and radiation protection.
  5. Clinical trainingSupervised hospital training, then work as a medical physicist. A PhD opens research.

For teachers

Running a session at school or an open day.

Session plan

  • Each chapter takes 10–15 minutes and stands alone. The physics matches SPM, Cambridge O level and IGCSE Physics.
  • Chapter 1 and 6 pair well for a 30-minute taster: bunker shielding, then protons.
  • Every level ends with the physics it used and a real job title.

Syllabus links (SPM, O level, IGCSE)

  • Ch 1: radioactivity, absorption, inverse square law
  • Ch 2: X-rays, exponential attenuation
  • Ch 3: measurement, error, gas laws
  • Ch 4: magnetism and waves
  • Ch 5: half-life and decay
  • Ch 6: energy and charged particles
