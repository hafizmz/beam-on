Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026. Given under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia), as amended in 2024.

EnglishBahasa Malaysia

This policy covers the Beam On! games: Beam On!, Beam On! Junior, Beam On: First Light and Beam On! Explorace, both on this website and as apps from Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Who is responsible

The games are made and run by Mohd Hafiz Mohd Zin (Hafiz M Zin), Associate Professor in Medical Physics, Pusat Kanser Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi, Universiti Sains Malaysia. Contact: hafiz.zin@physics.org.

The apps from Google Play and the App Store

The Beam On! and Beam On! Junior apps do not collect, send or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Names, pictures, marks and game progress stay on your device only. They are removed when you delete the app or use "Reset my marks" in Junior.

In the Junior app, links that leave the app are behind a question for grown-ups.

The games on this website

When you play on this website, some games send a small amount of data to the organiser's Google Forms or Google Sheets so we can see how the outreach programme is used.

Game progress, saved departments and Junior player names and pictures are kept only in your own browser (local storage).

Beam On! and Beam On! Junior use fonts stored on this website. Some other pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device's internet address when those pages open. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Data sent from the website goes to Google Forms and Google Sheets owned by the organiser. Google's servers may be outside Malaysia. Only the organiser can see the responses. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else. Reports only show anonymous totals.

How long we keep it

Registrations and play data are kept until 31 December 2027 and then deleted. Anonymous totals may be kept longer.

Children

Beam On! Junior is made for children aged 4 to 12 and should be played with a parent or teacher nearby. The Junior website sends only anonymous play data, never names or pictures. The Junior app sends nothing at all. Beam On! registration is meant for players aged 13 and over; younger players should ask a parent or teacher.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your registration details, or withdraw your consent, by writing to hafiz.zin@physics.org. A parent or guardian can do this for their child. You can remove everything saved on your device by clearing the site data in your browser, using "Reset my marks" in Junior, or deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Aplikasi dari Google Play dan App Store

Aplikasi Beam On! dan Beam On! Junior tidak mengumpul, menghantar atau berkongsi sebarang data peribadi. Tiada akaun, tiada pendaftaran, tiada iklan, tiada analitik dan tiada penjejakan. Nama, gambar, markah dan kemajuan permainan kekal pada peranti anda sahaja, dan dipadam apabila aplikasi dipadam atau apabila anda menekan "Set semula markah saya" dalam Junior.

Dalam aplikasi Junior, pautan yang keluar dari aplikasi dilindungi dengan soalan untuk orang dewasa.

Permainan di laman web ini

  • Beam On!: jika anda mendaftar, nama penuh, e-mel, institusi atau sekolah, peranan, negara dan persetujuan anda dihantar ke Borang Google penganjur. Kiraan main tanpa nama juga dihantar: kod pelawat rawak, peristiwa, saat bermain, hari permainan dan versi. Tiada nama atau butiran hubungan.
  • Beam On! Junior: data main tanpa nama sahaja: umur (4 hingga 12), mod, bahasa, bilik, markah, masa diambil, jenis peranti dan kod rawak. Nama dan gambar tidak pernah dihantar.
  • Beam On: First Light: lihat notis privasi First Light.
  • Beam On! Explorace: tiada data dihantar.

Data disimpan dalam Borang Google dan Google Sheets milik penganjur. Pelayan Google mungkin berada di luar Malaysia. Hanya penganjur boleh melihatnya. Kami tidak menjual atau berkongsi data anda, dan tidak menggunakannya untuk iklan. Data disimpan sehingga 31 Disember 2027 dan kemudian dipadam. Untuk melihat, membetulkan atau memadam butiran anda, tulis kepada hafiz.zin@physics.org. Ibu bapa atau penjaga boleh berbuat demikian bagi pihak anak mereka.
